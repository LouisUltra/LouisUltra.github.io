Semantic Indexing for Agentic Code Localization in Large Repositories (Code Atlas)

ICSE 2027 (CCF-A) · under review · double-blind

Product-style natural-language queries fail to locate code in industrial repositories — the best commercial agents drop from 96%+ to 52.9% at scale. A three-layer Graph-RAG method: dependency-topological semantic indexing with bilingual AST-validated descriptions, HAC module clustering, and staged retrieval. Top-1 (W-R@5) across all five benchmarked repositories up to 49K files; 97.9% on SWE-bench Verified; 34 controlled ablations. Patent filed (first inventor); piloted on real business modules.

Outcome-Conditioned End-Effector Geometry Across Vision-Language-Action Policies

ICRA 2027 · under review · double-blind

Task success alone does not establish whether VLA policies physically execute alike. Cross-policy end-effector geometry over 15,000 closed-loop LIBERO rollouts from four policies (OpenVLA / OpenVLA-OFT / UniVLA / π0.5): both-success pairs sit at a median DTW of 0.0120 m versus 0.0380 m when exactly one succeeds — an ordering that holds across every task, pair and representation. A matched comparison design: outcome strata, configuration-matched pairs, within-policy baselines, demonstration references, fixed windows, alignment sensitivity.

Collapse, Not Complexity: Failure-Conditioned Decomposition Repair for End-to-End Document Parsing

ICASSP 2027 · under review · with advisor

Complexity is the wrong decision variable for document-VLM reasoning modes: reasoning lowers mean quality (−2.21 Overall) at 1.54× tokens, and pre-inference features cannot predict its benefit (AUROC 0.47). Benefit concentrates on pages whose ordinary pass has collapsed — a repetition pathology, not layout difficulty. A reference-free trigger on the already-paid decoding trace, followed by projection-based decomposition and region re-parsing: +1.40 Overall at 1.13× tokens, replicating across 3B–8B backbones; +2.41 (CI [1.64, 3.46]) on 1,175 held-out benchmark pages with all parameters frozen.

From PDF to Evidence: Structure-Aware Retrieval for Clinical Practice Guidelines

IEEE BIBM 2026 · under review · sole author · double-blind

Clinical guidelines are published as unstructured PDFs whose tables, flowcharts and graded recommendations carry the key evidence. Structure-aware evidence units — sections, table rows, flowchart paths, graded recommendations with preserved headers and branch labels — achieve the best element-level localization across BM25 / Dense / Hybrid retrievers (Hit@1 0.382) at 3.8× less context than page-level retrieval, on a 26-document, 16-specialty corpus with 199 annotated queries (p < 0.005).
