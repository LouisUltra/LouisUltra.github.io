ICSE 2027 CCF-A double-blind

Semantic Indexing for Agentic Code Localization in Large Repositories

Code Atlas · patent filed, first inventor
top-1 on all 5 benchmarked repositories · 97.9% SWE-bench Verified

Product-style natural-language queries fail to locate code in industrial repositories — the strongest commercial agents fall from 96%+ to 52.9% at scale, grep-based baselines to 1.3%. A three-layer Graph-RAG method: dependency-topological semantic indexing with bilingual, AST-validated descriptions; HAC module clustering; staged retrieval. Established over 34 controlled ablations; piloted on real business modules.

ICRA 2027 double-blind

Outcome-Conditioned End-Effector Geometry Across Vision-Language-Action Policies

15,000 closed-loop LIBERO rollouts · four policies
median DTW 0.0120 m (both-success) vs 0.0380 m (mixed)

Task success alone does not establish whether VLA policies execute alike. Cross-policy end-effector geometry over 15,000 closed-loop rollouts of OpenVLA, OpenVLA-OFT, UniVLA and π0.5: both-success pairs sit far closer than mixed-outcome pairs — an ordering that holds across every task, pair and representation examined. A matched comparison design throughout: outcome strata, configuration-matched pairs, within-policy baselines, demonstration references.

ICASSP 2027 with advisor

Collapse, Not Complexity: Failure-Conditioned Decomposition Repair for End-to-End Document Parsing

with Dehui Du (advisor) · 3B–8B backbones
+1.40 Overall at 1.13× tokens · +2.41 on 1,175 held-out pages

Complexity is the wrong decision variable for document-VLM reasoning modes: reasoning lowers mean quality at 1.54× tokens, and pre-inference features cannot predict its benefit (AUROC 0.47). Benefit concentrates where the ordinary pass has already collapsed — a repetition pathology, not layout difficulty. A reference-free trigger on the already-paid decoding trace, then projection-based decomposition and region re-parsing; all parameters frozen on held-out evaluation.

IEEE BIBM 2026sole author

From PDF to Evidence: Structure-Aware Retrieval for Clinical Practice Guidelines

26 guidelines · 199 annotated queries
best element-level localization · Hit@1 0.382 at 3.8× less context

Clinical guidelines ship as unstructured PDFs whose tables, flowcharts and graded recommendations carry the key evidence. Structure-aware evidence units — sections, table rows, flowchart paths, graded recommendations with preserved headers and branch labels — outperform fixed-chunking and page-level retrieval across BM25 / Dense / Hybrid retrievers (p < 0.005).
