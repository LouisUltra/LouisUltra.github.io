ICSE 2027 CCF-A double-blind

Semantic Indexing for Agentic Code Localization in Large Repositories

Code Atlas · under review · patent filed, first inventor · piloted on real business modules

Product-style natural-language queries fail to locate code in industrial repositories — the strongest commercial agents fall from 96%+ to 52.9% at scale, grep-based baselines to 1.3%. A three-layer Graph-RAG method: dependency-topological semantic indexing with bilingual, AST-validated descriptions; HAC module clustering; staged retrieval. Top-1 (W-R@5) on all five benchmarked repositories up to 49K files, 97.9% on SWE-bench Verified, established over 34 controlled ablations.

ICRA 2027 double-blind

Outcome-Conditioned End-Effector Geometry Across Vision-Language-Action Policies

under review · 15,000 closed-loop LIBERO rollouts · four policies

Task success alone does not establish whether VLA policies execute alike. Cross-policy end-effector geometry over 15,000 closed-loop rollouts of OpenVLA, OpenVLA-OFT, UniVLA and π0.5: both-success pairs sit at a median DTW of 0.0120 m versus 0.0380 m when exactly one succeeds — an ordering that holds across every task, pair and representation examined. A matched comparison design throughout: outcome strata, configuration-matched pairs, within-policy baselines, demonstration references.

ICASSP 2027 with advisor

Collapse, Not Complexity: Failure-Conditioned Decomposition Repair for End-to-End Document Parsing

under review · with Dehui Du (advisor) · East China Normal University

Complexity is the wrong decision variable for document-VLM reasoning modes: reasoning lowers mean quality by 2.21 Overall at 1.54× tokens, and pre-inference features cannot predict its benefit (AUROC 0.47). Benefit concentrates where the ordinary pass has already collapsed — a repetition pathology, not layout difficulty. A reference-free trigger on the already-paid decoding trace, then projection-based decomposition and region re-parsing: +1.40 Overall at 1.13× tokens, replicating across 3B–8B backbones; +2.41 (95% CI [1.64, 3.46]) on 1,175 held-out pages, parameters frozen.

IEEE BIBM 2026 · sole author

From PDF to Evidence: Structure-Aware Retrieval for Clinical Practice Guidelines

under review · sole author · 26 guidelines · 199 annotated queries

Clinical guidelines ship as unstructured PDFs whose tables, flowcharts and graded recommendations carry the key evidence. Structure-aware evidence units — sections, table rows, flowchart paths, graded recommendations with preserved headers and branch labels — achieve the best element-level localization across BM25 / Dense / Hybrid retrievers (Hit@1 0.382) at 3.8× less context than page-level retrieval (p < 0.005).
